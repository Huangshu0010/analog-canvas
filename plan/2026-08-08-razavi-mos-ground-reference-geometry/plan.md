## Shared Dependencies

- MOS four-terminal and three-terminal variants share the same base geometry.
- `D/G/S/B` anchors must remain on the 10-unit connection grid.
- Generated assets, catalog hashes and fidelity boards must be regenerated by
  their owner scripts; do not hand-edit generated output.
- The supplied image is a visual calibration reference, not a connectivity
  source or a replacement for the binary Visio stencil.

## Expected Work

1. Measure the reference's normalized MOS/Ground ratios and compare them with
   the current generated geometry. Retain the existing 1.15 gate-axis
   calibration; do not elongate that axis further. Apply a 0.765 symmetric
   S/D-axis contraction to the MOS body, including the rows on which the
   source/drain horizontal spans sit, while preserving their attachment
   endpoints to the gate/channel and all electrical pin anchors. Restore all
   drain/source spans and arrow shafts to their original stroke presentation.
   Render only the two semantic gate-bar shapes as sharp-cornered, filled
   3.24-unit rectangles (1.5× their original 2.16-unit emphasis width),
   independent of the instance's display rotation. The
   target Ground retains 1 : 0.5 : 0.25 bar widths and the calibrated 1.18
   lead-axis scale.
2. Encode the reference-calibrated geometry in source generators while keeping
   pin anchors and three-terminal visual-only semantics intact.
3. Restore the source-derived PMOS three-terminal branch presentation and
   shorten the VDD built-in symbol's vertical stem by a further 30% (25 to
   17.5 units) rather than its horizontal bar. Render the VDD horizontal bar
   as a sharp-cornered, filled 3.24-unit rectangle; add regression checks for
   both contracts.
4. Regenerate catalog assets and formal/fidelity goldens, then add regression
   checks for the calibrated dimensions.
5. Move DC voltage-source polarity marks outside the circle as fixed
   sharp-stroke primitives, and recalibrate the DC current-source arrow to a
   shorter shaft with a wider triangular head while preserving pin anchors and
   the source circle.
